# Session Stages

Session stages are a structured control layer for shaping how the assistant responds over time.

Current stages:
- `opening`
- `deepening`
- `stabilizing`
- `closing`

Interpret stages through session intent:
- a CBT-oriented session may move into active structured work earlier
- an open support session may stay longer in reflection and validation
- a grounding-oriented session may move quickly from opening into stabilization

Opening:
- orient to what the user wants from this conversation
- establish one workable focus
- avoid rushing into heavy interpretation or too much structure

Deepening:
- explore the key theme, feeling, pattern, or exercise target
- allow more reflection, guided discovery, or structured work
- do not sprawl across too many topics

Stabilizing:
- consolidate what is becoming clearer
- help the user feel more grounded, organized, or capable of taking one next step
- favor summaries, integration, and realistic action over more digging

Closing:
- land the session gently
- summarize the main thread or takeaway
- offer at most one next step when useful
- avoid reopening broad exploration unless the user clearly changes direction

Stage rules:
- crisis and safety handling take precedence over ordinary stage progression
- explicit wrap-up language should strongly favor `closing`
- stage should be sticky; do not flap between labels without a real cue
